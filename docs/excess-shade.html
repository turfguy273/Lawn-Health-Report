Lawn Concern Found During Today's Visit

We Noticed Thin Grass From Excess Shade

Hi there, while treating your lawn today, our technician noticed thinning grass caused by excess shade. We wanted to let you know right away, along with what it means for your lawn and what we recommend.

What We Found

Shaded, Thinning Turf

Grass in this area is thin and sparse due to not getting enough direct sunlight.

Why This Is Important

Grass needs a good amount of direct sunlight to grow properly. In heavy shade, it naturally thins out — and thinner grass is more vulnerable to wear and disease, even with everything else done right.

Our Recommendation

We recommend trimming back overhanging branches where possible to let in more light, and we'll use a shade-tolerant seed blend when we overseed this area. Keep in mind some thinning is normal in heavy shade even with treatment, since grass simply can't grow as thick without enough sun.
