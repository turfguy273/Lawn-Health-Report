Lawn Concern Found During Today's Visit

We Noticed Your Lawn Is Being Mowed Too Short

Hi there, while treating your lawn today, our technician noticed signs of scalping — grass cut shorter than it should be. We wanted to let you know right away, along with what it means for your lawn and what we recommend.

What We Found

Mowed Too Short (Scalping)

Brown or yellow stressed grass right after a mow, stems and crowns visibly exposed, and sometimes weeds moving into the thinned area — that's exactly what our technician found in your lawn today.

Cause
Mower height too low
Appearance
Yellow-brown blades
Pattern
Follows mower lines
Recovery
1–2 weeks if corrected
What This Means For You

Cutting grass too short removes more of the leaf blade than the plant can afford, which cuts into its ability to photosynthesize and shocks the root system. It also opens up bare soil where weeds are quick to move in.

Our Recommendation

We highly recommend mowing Fescue turf at a minimum of 4 inches, and Bermuda turf at 2.5–3 inches. Cutting at the right height for your grass type lets it recover on its own and stay healthier going forward — no treatment needed, just a mowing height adjustment.
