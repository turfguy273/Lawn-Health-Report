What We Found

Dallisgrass

This coarse, fast-growing weed grows outward from a single point, forming a circular clump with wider, paler blades than the surrounding turf. Within days of mowing it shoots up tall seed stalks that make it even more noticeable — and that's exactly what our technician found in your lawn today.

In Fescue lawns

Fescue is already a coarser, clump-forming grass, so Dallisgrass can blend in more from a distance — but its faster vertical growth between mowings and slightly yellower tone still give it away.

What This Means For You

Why It's Worth Addressing Now

Dallisgrass is one of the toughest lawn weeds to fully eliminate. It spreads through both seed and short rhizomes, competes aggressively with your turf for water and nutrients, and its deep root system means a single routine treatment won't clear it. Left alone, it will keep coming back in the same spot and can spread to new areas of your yard.

What We'll Do About It

Our Plan

Because Dallisgrass needs a more targeted approach than routine treatments, we're going to send you an estimate for a treatment plan to get rid of it for good. Getting ahead of it now will keep it from spreading further and save you time down the road.
